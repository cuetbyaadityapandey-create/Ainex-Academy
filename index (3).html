AINEX COMPUTER ACADEMY


AINEX Computer Academy is a professional computer education institute dedicated to providing quality technical education, practical training and career-oriented skills. Our mission is to make every student digitally skilled and career-ready through affordable computer education, practical learning and modern technology. We provide CCC, DCA, ADCA, Tally Prime + GST, Advanced Excel, Hindi Typing, English Typing, Web Designing, O Level, PGDCA, CPCT and many more professional courses.
